# Code of Conduct

reelset follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and no harassment of any
kind. This applies in issues, discussions, pull requests and any other reelset community space.

To report a problem, use [a private report](https://github.com/reelset/reelset/security/advisories/new)
or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content
or ban accounts that break these rules.
